# Gotcha — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Gotcha does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Everything stays on your device.** The only thing Gotcha saves is which affirmations you've already drawn, so the deck can shuffle without repeats. It's stored locally on your iPhone and never leaves it.
- **No accounts.** Gotcha has no sign-up or login.
- **No tracking or analytics.** Gotcha doesn't use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests.

Deleting the app removes all of its data.
